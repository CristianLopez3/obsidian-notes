
**Amazon Route 53** is a domain name service or also known as **DNS** 


**DNS**: acts as a translation services → Translates Internet Protocols IPs into human readable names - because human read words and computers (nets) reads IPs

* www.google.com = 192.182.0.1 or something like this.

### Routing Policies

* Latency-based routing
* Geolocation DNS
* Weighted round robin
