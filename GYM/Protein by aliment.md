## Meat

| **Alimento** | **Protein in Grams** | Notes                                                                                |
| ------------ | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| Chicken 🐔   | 28 to 30             | It's the meat with the highest protein concentration and the meat with the less fat. |
